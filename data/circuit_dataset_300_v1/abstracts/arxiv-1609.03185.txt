A quantum algorithm to solve the parity problem is better than its most efficient classical counterpart with a separation that is polynomial in the number of queries. This was shown by E. Bernstein and U. Vazirani and was one of the earliest indications that the quantum information processing can outperform the classical one by a significant margin. The problem and its solution both is usually stated for a 2-level system since we generally work with bits/qubits. However, many works have been done generalizing known quantum computing techniques to higher level systems. Following this, we look at a generalization of the Bernstein-Vazirani algorithm implemented on a general qudit system.
