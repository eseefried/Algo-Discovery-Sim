Use invariant subspace (Lanczos) reduction of continuous-time quantum walks on symmetric graphs to obtain the reduced state set confining the dynamics, and apply it to spatial search (complete graphs with broken links, complete bipartite/star graphs), quantum transport efficiency, and an upper bound on single-qubit transfer fidelity on an XY spin network.

Scope: Conceptual reconstruction of the Lanczos-based invariant-subspace reduction for a continuous-time quantum walk, with a tiny finite illustrative instance of spatial search. Specific graph sizes, results and numerical values are not in the abstract.

## Symbols

- $H$: Hamiltonian of the walk on the graph (adjacency or Laplacian, plus oracle term for search); domain: Hermitian operator on N-dimensional Hilbert space
- $|\psi_0\rangle$: initial state seeding the Krylov subspace; domain: unit vector in C^N
- $\mathcal{K}_m$: invariant (Krylov) subspace generated by Lanczos; domain: subspace of dimension m <= N
- $|q_k\rangle$: Lanczos basis vectors; domain: orthonormal vectors, k=0..m-1

## eq_1: Krylov subspace

$$
\mathcal{K}_m=\mathrm{span}\{|\psi_0\rangle,H|\psi_0\rangle,\dots,H^{m-1}|\psi_0\rangle\}
$$

Invariant subspace containing the dynamics from the initial state.

Provenance: `standard_background`. Standard definition underlying Lanczos; abstract only mentions invariant subspace methods computed via Lanczos.

## eq_2: Lanczos recurrence

$$
H|q_k\rangle=b_{k+1}|q_{k+1}\rangle+a_k|q_k\rangle+b_k|q_{k-1}\rangle
$$

Three-term recurrence making H tridiagonal in the reduced basis.

Provenance: `standard_background`. Standard Lanczos algorithm; coefficients not given in abstract.

## eq_3: Reduced dynamics

$$
|\psi(t)\rangle=e^{-iHt}|\psi_0\rangle=\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}c_k(t)|q_k\rangle
$$

The walk evolves within the m-dimensional reduced subspace.

Provenance: `abstract_inferred`. Formalizes the confinement of dynamics to a subspace.

Abstract evidence: confine the dynamics to a smaller subspace of the full Hilbert space

## Measurement

For spatial search, measure in the vertex basis at an evolution time t and take the probability of the marked vertex, |c_target(t)|^2 from the reduced amplitudes. For transport, the efficiency is the population absorbed at a sink or target; for XY spin transfer, the fidelity is the overlap on the target site. Exact observables are assumed, not given in the abstract.

## Classical postprocessing

Scan t to find the time maximizing success probability, transport efficiency or fidelity. Compare across graphs (e.g. with links removed) and scaling with N to judge optimality. The fidelity upper bound is derived analytically in the reduced space (not specified in the abstract).

## Algorithm steps

1. Choose graph Hamiltonian H (with oracle term for search) and seed state |psi_0>. (eq_1)
2. Run Lanczos from |psi_0> to build orthonormal basis |q_k> and tridiagonal reduced H of dimension m. (eq_1, eq_2)
3. Evolve under e^{-iHt} in the reduced basis, tracking amplitudes c_k(t). (eq_3)
