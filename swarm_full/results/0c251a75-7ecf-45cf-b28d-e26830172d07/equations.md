# Abstract to equations

Status: **partial**

Model: `external:ledger_swarm`

Mathematics has not been independently verified.

Create an efficient transport channel from a fixed source site A to a fixed target site B in a disordered 2D discrete-time quantum walk by cutting links along a single line connecting A to B. The channel is carried by topologically protected chiral edge states, despite a vanishing bulk Chern number. The walk is realized as a periodically driven lattice Hamiltonian, and the relevant bulk invariant is the quasienergy winding.

Scope: Abstract-level reconstruction of the walk dynamics and the topological invariant, with a small finite lattice instance as an illustrative assumption. The paper's specific coin, shift and disorder model, cut geometry and numerical results are not reconstructed.

## Symbols

- $U$: One-step discrete-time quantum walk (Floquet) operator; domain: unitary on lattice-site (position) tensor coin Hilbert space
- $\varepsilon$: Quasienergy of U; domain: real, defined modulo 2\pi
- $H_F$: Effective Floquet Hamiltonian for the periodically driven lattice; domain: Hermitian operator
- $W$: Quasienergy winding number, the bulk topological invariant; domain: integer
- $C$: Bulk Chern number; domain: integer; equals 0 here

## eq_1: Floquet operator

$$
U=e^{-iH_F T}
$$

The one-step walk operator is a unitary generated by the periodically driven Hamiltonian over period T. The quasienergy is defined by this relation.

Provenance: `standard_background`. Standard Floquet relation. The abstract states the walk is realized as a periodically driven lattice Hamiltonian, but not this form.

Equation assumptions:

- Period T; H_F is the effective Hamiltonian from the log of U

W\neq 0\ \Rightarrow\ \text{chiral edge states along the cut}

A nonzero quasienergy winding is the bulk invariant responsible for the chiral edge states that carry transport along the cut line.

Provenance: `abstract_inferred`. The abstract names the winding as the responsible invariant. The explicit winding formula is not given.

## Measurement

## Classical postprocessing

Average the estimated P_B(t) over disorder realizations and repeated shots. Compare cut-line against uncut configurations to assess channel efficiency, e.g. via maximum or time-resolved P_B(t). The abstract does not specify the observable.

## Algorithm steps

1. Prepare the walker at site A with the initial coin state. (no equation reference)
2. Apply the coin operation at each site (assumed, possibly with static disorder in coin angles). (eq_1)
3. Apply the conditional shift along lattice links, omitting shifts across links cut along the line from A to B (assumed reflection or no hopping at cuts). (eq_1)
4. Repeat the one-step operator U for t steps; transport along the cut is expected to follow chiral edge states. (eq_1, eq_3)
