# Abstract to equations

Status: **partial**

Model: `claude-sonnet-5-5`

Mathematics has not been independently verified.

Exact synthesis of single-qubit unitaries over the Clifford-cyclotomic gate set (Clifford group plus z-rotation by pi/n, n positive even), minimizing the number of pi/n z-rotations; specialized here to the Clifford+T case n=4 as a tiny instance.

Scope: Only the ring characterization of candidate exactly synthesizable unitaries and the generic T-count-reducing step structure for n=4 (Clifford+T). The paper's general-n algorithm, its minimality proof, and the proof that the ring characterization fails for 100% of even n are outside scope and not reconstructed.

## Symbols

- $n$: even positive integer defining the rotation angle pi/n; domain: 2Z_{>0}
- $\omega_n$: e^{i\pi/n}, primitive 2n-th root of unity; domain: complex unit circle
- $R_n$: ring Z[e^{i\pi/n},1/2]; domain: subring of C
- $U$: single-qubit unitary; domain: U(2)
- $R_z(\theta)$: z-rotation diag(e^{-i\theta/2},e^{i\theta/2}); domain: U(2)
- $\mathcal{C}$: single-qubit Clifford group; domain: finite subgroup of U(2)
- $k$: number of pi/n z-rotations (T-count for n=4); domain: nonnegative integer

## eq_1: Ring of entries

$$
R_n=\mathbb{Z}[e^{i\pi/n},1/2]
$$

Ring whose unitaries were shown to coincide with exactly synthesizable ones for n=4.

Provenance: `abstract_explicit`. Ring is named in the abstract.

Abstract evidence: the group of unitaries with entries over the ring $\mathbb{Z}[e^{i\frac{\pi}{n}},1/2]$

## eq_2: Gate set

$$
\mathcal{G}_n=\langle \mathcal{C},\,R_z(\pi/n)\rangle
$$

Clifford-cyclotomic gate set generated by the Clifford group and the pi/n z-rotation.

Provenance: `abstract_explicit`. Direct description of the gate set; the generated-group notation is mine.

Abstract evidence: the Clifford group plus a $z$-rotation by $\frac{\pi}{n}$

Equation assumptions:

- Gates are considered up to global phase conventions not specified in the abstract.

## eq_3: Exact synthesis form

$$
U=C_k\prod_{j=1}^{k}\left(R_z(\pi/n)\,C_{j-1}\right)\ \text{up to ordering},\quad C_j\in\mathcal{C},\ k\ \text{minimal}
$$

Decomposition with k pi/n rotations interleaved with Cliffords, k minimal.

Provenance: `abstract_inferred`. Inferred from the minimality claim; the exact decomposition form is a generic assumption.

Abstract evidence: outputs a decomposition using the minimum number of $\frac{\pi}{n}$ $z$-rotations

Equation assumptions:

- Product ordering convention is a choice.

## eq_4: Characterization for n=4

$$
\{U\in U(2): U\ \text{exactly synthesizable over Clifford+T}\}=U(2)\cap M_2(R_4)
$$

For n=4 exactly synthesizable unitaries equal unitaries with entries in R_4; equality up to global phase conventions is not specified.

Provenance: `abstract_explicit`. Stated for n=4 in the abstract.

Abstract evidence: the group of exactly synthesizable unitaries was shown to be equal to the group of unitaries with entries over the ring

Equation assumptions:

- Phase normalization as in the original Clifford+T result.

## eq_5: Unitarity constraint

$$
U^\dagger U=I,\quad U=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix},\ a,b,c,d\in R_n
$$

Unitarity condition on matrices with ring entries.

Provenance: `standard_background`. Standard definition of unitary with entries in a ring closed under needed conjugation.

Equation assumptions:

- R_n is closed under complex conjugation.

## Classical postprocessing

Classical algorithm outputs the gate sequence; its details are not given in the abstract.

## Algorithm steps

1. Given U with entries in R_n, check unitarity. (eq_1, eq_5)
2. Iteratively multiply by Clifford and R_z(pi/n) gates to reduce a ring-based complexity measure (for n=4, the denominator exponent), until a Clifford remains; the reduction rule for general n is not given in the abstract. (eq_2, eq_3)
3. For n=4, the exactly synthesizable set equals unitaries over R_4. (eq_4)

## Assumptions

- Chose Clifford+T (n=4) as the tiny instance for illustration.
- Decomposition form in eq_3 is generic, not the paper's.
