## eq_1: Toffoli action

$$
U_T|a,b,c\rangle=|a,b,c\oplus(a\wedge b)\rangle,\quad a,b,c\in\{0,1\}
$$

Toffoli flips the target qubit c iff both controls a and b equal 1.

Provenance: `standard_background`. Definition of the Toffoli (CCNOT) gate; the abstract names it but does not write the relation.

Equation assumptions:

- Qubit order (a,b,c) with c as target

## eq_2: Time-ordered propagator

$$
U(T;\theta)=\mathcal{T}\exp\!\left(-\frac{i}{\hbar}\int_0^T H(t;\theta)\,dt\right)
$$

Unitary produced by the controlled Hamiltonian.

Provenance: `standard_background`. Standard quantum control; the specific H and controls are not given in the abstract.

Equation assumptions:

- Closed-system evolution

## eq_3: Generic transmon chain Hamiltonian (placeholder)

$$
H(t)=\sum_{j=1}^{3}\left[\omega_j b_j^\dagger b_j+\frac{\alpha_j}{2}b_j^\dagger b_j^\dagger b_j b_j+\epsilon_j(t)(b_j+b_j^\dagger)\right]+\sum_{j=1}^{2}g_{j}(b_j^\dagger b_{j+1}+b_j b_{j+1}^\dagger)
$$

Typical Duffing-oscillator model of three nearest-neighbor-coupled transmons with drive controls.

Provenance: `assumption`. The abstract only says three nearest-neighbor-coupled transmons; this form is a common model, not confirmed as the paper's.

Equation assumptions:

- Duffing model
- Exchange coupling between neighbors only
- Drive controls \epsilon_j(t) are unspecified

## eq_4: Gate fidelity

$$
F(\theta)=\frac{\left|\mathrm{Tr}\!\left(U_T^\dagger P\,U(T;\theta)P\right)\right|^2}{d^2},\quad d=8
$$

Trace-overlap fidelity on the computational subspace projected by P; the optimization target is F≥0.999.

Provenance: `assumption`. The abstract gives only the 99.9% figure; this fidelity definition is a common choice and may differ from the paper's.

Equation assumptions:

- P projects onto the 8-dim computational subspace
- Leakage penalized via the projection

## eq_5: Optimization objective

$$
\theta^\star=\arg\min_{\theta}\,[1-F(\theta)]
$$

DE searches the control parameter space to maximize fidelity at fixed T.

Provenance: `abstract_inferred`. DE-based control implies minimizing an infidelity cost; the enhancements and non-greedy selection rule are not specified.

Abstract evidence: The gate is achieved by a non-greedy quantum control procedure using our enhanced version of the Differential Evolution algorithm.

Equation assumptions:

- Single-objective cost
- T fixed, set to a two-qubit-gate-comparable duration
