# Abstract to equations

Status: **partial**

Model: `claude-sonnet-5-5`

Mathematics has not been independently verified.

Approximate a single-qubit Markovian channel T_t=e^{tL} generated by a Lindbladian L to accuracy epsilon, using single-qubit and CNOT gates with one ancilla qubit.

Scope: Only the abstract-stated target channel, the stated gate-count scaling, and a generic background Trotter-like decomposition and recombination skeleton with a one-ancilla Stinespring dilation. The specific channel-approximation method, the decomposition of L and the recombination rule are outside scope.

## Symbols

- $T_t$: single-qubit quantum channel at time t; domain: CPTP maps on 2x2 density matrices
- $\mathcal{L}$: generator of the quantum dynamical semigroup; domain: linear maps on M_2(C)
- $t$: evolution time; domain: real, t>=0
- $\epsilon$: target accuracy; domain: real, 0<epsilon<1
- $\|\mathcal{L}\|_{(1\to1)}$: induced trace-norm (1->1) norm of generator; domain: nonnegative real
- $N$: gate count; domain: positive integer
- $r$: number of time slices (assumed); domain: positive integer
- $\mathcal{L}_j$: pieces in assumed decomposition L=sum_j L_j; domain: generators of single-qubit channels
- $V$: isometry/unitary dilating a channel with one ancilla; domain: unitary on 2 qubits

## eq_1: Target semigroup channel

$$
T_t=e^{t\mathcal{L}}
$$

Channel to be approximated.

Provenance: `abstract_explicit`. Stated directly in the abstract.

Abstract evidence: approximates the channel $T_{t}=e^{t\mathcal{L}}$ up to chosen accuracy $\epsilon$

## eq_2: Gate complexity

$$
N=\mathcal{O}\!\left(\frac{(\|\mathcal{L}\|_{(1\to1)}t)^{3/2}}{\epsilon^{1/2}}\right)
$$

Stated scaling of single-qubit and CNOT gates.

Provenance: `abstract_explicit`. Stated in the abstract.

Abstract evidence: $\mathcal{O}\big((||\mathcal{L}||_{(1\rightarrow 1)}t)^{3/2}/\epsilon^{1/2}\big)$ single qubit and CNOT gates

## eq_3: Accuracy requirement

$$
\|\tilde T_t-T_t\|\le\epsilon
$$

Approximation error of the implemented channel in some norm, probably diamond norm.

Provenance: `abstract_inferred`. The abstract gives an accuracy epsilon but not the norm; the diamond norm is assumed.

Abstract evidence: approximates the channel $T_{t}=e^{t\mathcal{L}}$ up to chosen accuracy $\epsilon$

Equation assumptions:

- Norm is the diamond norm

## eq_4: Semigroup slicing and decomposition

$$
e^{t\mathcal{L}}=\left(e^{(t/r)\mathcal{L}}\right)^r,\quad \mathcal{L}=\sum_j\mathcal{L}_j,\quad e^{(t/r)\mathcal{L}}\approx\prod_j e^{(t/r)\mathcal{L}_j}
$$

Generic product-formula skeleton consistent with the decompose-and-recombine idea; not the paper's actual decomposition.

Provenance: `standard_background`. Standard Lie-Trotter structure for semigroups; the abstract only says a decomposition and recombination technique is used.

Equation assumptions:

- The paper's decomposition may differ

## eq_5: One-ancilla dilation

$$
T(\rho)=\mathrm{Tr}_{A}\!\left[V(\rho\otimes|0\rangle\langle0|_A)V^\dagger\right]
$$

Single-qubit channel realised by a unitary on system plus one ancilla qubit.

Provenance: `standard_background`. Stinespring dilation. Qubit channels with at most 2 Kraus operators need one ancilla; the abstract states one ancilla suffices.

Equation assumptions:

- The ancilla is reset or fresh for each channel application

## Registers

- S: 1 qubits; system qubit
- A: 1 qubits; single ancilla qubit for dilation

## Initial state

System in an arbitrary input state rho; ancilla in |0>.

## Algorithm steps

1. Fix the target T_t=e^{tL}, the accuracy epsilon and the norm. (eq_1, eq_3)
2. Decompose the evolution into short-time pieces and recombine them as a product (the paper's specific decomposition is not given). (eq_4)
3. Implement each piece as a single-qubit channel via a one-ancilla dilation compiled to single-qubit and CNOT gates, using the unspecified channel approximation methods. (eq_5, eq_2)

## Assumptions

- Diamond norm is assumed for epsilon.
- The product-formula skeleton is generic background, not the paper's method.
- Ancilla reuse or reset between slices is assumed.
