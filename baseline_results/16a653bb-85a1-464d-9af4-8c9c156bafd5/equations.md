# Abstract to equations

Status: **partial**

Model: `claude-fable-5-1`

Mathematics has not been independently verified.

Reconstruct a mathematical specification for a single-shot (non-decomposed) Toffoli (CCNOT) gate on three nearest-neighbor-coupled superconducting transmons, obtained by optimal quantum control with a target fidelity of 99.9% and a duration comparable to an entangling two-qubit gate. The abstract gives the target gate, the physical platform, the fidelity target, and the optimizer name, but no Hamiltonian, pulse parametrization, control schedule, or fidelity definition.

Scope: Smallest useful construction (chosen as an assumption since no experiment_scope was supplied): (i) the ideal 3-qubit Toffoli unitary as the target, with explicit register ordering |c_1 c_2 t> and integer encoding; (ii) a generic control-theoretic formulation (propagator from a parametrized Hamiltonian, gate-fidelity functional, maximization) with an assumed generic three-transmon chain Hamiltonian; (iii) a tiny verification instance: apply the gate to the 8 computational basis states and check the truth table (e.g. |110> -> |111>, |111> -> |110>, all others fixed). Outside scope: the paper's specific transmon parameters, pulse shapes, control-parameter set, the 'non-greedy' procedure, the enhanced Differential Evolution algorithm, leakage/decoherence modelling, and the numerical result (99.9%, gate time), none of which can be reconstructed from the abstract.

## Symbols

- $c_1, c_2, t$: Classical bit values of control qubit 1, control qubit 2 and target qubit in a computational basis state; domain: \{0,1\}
- $|c_1 c_2 t\rangle$: Three-qubit computational basis state; register order is (control 1, control 2, target) with c_1 the most significant bit, integer label x = 4c_1 + 2c_2 + t; domain: Basis of \mathbb{C}^8, x \in \{0,\dots,7\}
- $U_{\mathrm{Toff}}$: Ideal Toffoli (CCNOT) unitary, the target gate; domain: 8\times 8 unitary (permutation) matrix
- $X, I$: Single-qubit Pauli-X (NOT) and identity; domain: 2\times 2 unitary matrices
- $H(\tau;\vec{\theta})$: Time-dependent system-plus-control Hamiltonian of the three coupled transmons, depending on control parameters; domain: Hermitian operator on the truncated transmon Hilbert space, \hbar = 1
- $\vec{\theta}$: Vector of classical control parameters (e.g. pulse amplitudes/shapes/frequencies) optimized by Differential Evolution; dimension and meaning unspecified in abstract; domain: \mathbb{R}^{K}, K unknown
- $T$: Total gate duration; domain: T > 0
- $U(T;\vec{\theta})$: Time-ordered propagator generated by H over [0,T]; domain: Unitary on the transmon Hilbert space
- $P$: Projector from the physical (multi-level transmon) space onto the 8-dimensional computational subspace; domain: Orthogonal projector
- $d$: Dimension of the computational subspace; domain: d = 8
- $F(\vec{\theta})$: Gate fidelity between the realized propagator (projected to the computational subspace) and the ideal Toffoli; domain: [0,1]
- $a_j, a_j^\dagger, n_j$: Annihilation, creation and number operators of transmon j (assumed model only); domain: Operators on truncated oscillator space of transmon j, j \in \{1,2,3\}
- $\omega_j, \alpha_j, g_{j,j+1}, \Omega_j(\tau)$: Transmon frequency, anharmonicity, nearest-neighbor coupling, and drive amplitude of transmon j (assumed model only; values unknown); domain: Real numbers / real functions of time

## eq_1: Toffoli action on computational basis

$$
U_{\mathrm{Toff}}\,|c_1\, c_2\, t\rangle = |c_1,\; c_2,\; t \oplus (c_1 \wedge c_2)\rangle,\qquad c_1,c_2,t\in\{0,1\}
$$

The target qubit is flipped (XOR with 1) exactly when both controls are 1; this is exact, with \oplus denoting addition modulo 2. In integer encoding x = 4c_1+2c_2+t, the gate swaps x=6 and x=7 and fixes x=0,...,5.

Provenance: `standard_background`. Standard definition of the controlled-controlled-NOT gate; the abstract names the gate ('Toffoli, or controlled-controlled-NOT') but does not write its action.

Equation assumptions:

- Register ordering (c_1, c_2, t) with c_1 most significant is a convention chosen here.

## eq_2: Toffoli as controlled operator / matrix form

$$
U_{\mathrm{Toff}} = I\otimes I\otimes I - |11\rangle\langle 11|\otimes (I - X) = \sum_{x=0}^{5}|x\rangle\langle x| + |6\rangle\langle 7| + |7\rangle\langle 6|
$$

Operator form used as the reference unitary for fidelity evaluation and for an ideal-gate circuit baseline.

Provenance: `standard_background`. Direct operator restatement of eq_1.

Equation assumptions:

- Same register ordering as eq_1.

## eq_3: Controlled propagator

$$
U(T;\vec{\theta}) = \mathcal{T}\exp\!\left(-i\int_0^{T} H(\tau;\vec{\theta})\,d\tau\right)
$$

Time-ordered evolution generated by the controlled Hamiltonian over the gate duration; this is the object the quantum-control procedure shapes into a Toffoli.

Provenance: `abstract_inferred`. A 'single-shot' gate realized by quantum control means the Toffoli is implemented directly by one continuous controlled evolution rather than a gate sequence; the time-ordered exponential is the standard form of such an evolution (\hbar=1).

Abstract evidence: We develop a quantum-control procedure to construct a single-shot Toffoli gate

Equation assumptions:

- Closed-system (unitary) evolution; decoherence, if treated in the paper, is not modelled here.

## eq_4: Assumed three-transmon chain Hamiltonian (generic model)

$$
H(\tau;\vec{\theta}) = \sum_{j=1}^{3}\left[\omega_j n_j + \tfrac{\alpha_j}{2} n_j(n_j-1) + \Omega_j(\tau;\vec{\theta})\,(a_j + a_j^\dagger)\right] + \sum_{j=1}^{2} g_{j,j+1}\left(a_j^\dagger a_{j+1} + a_j a_{j+1}^\dagger\right)
$$

Generic Duffing-oscillator model of three transmons with nearest-neighbor exchange coupling and local drives; included only to make eq_3 concrete. The paper's actual Hamiltonian, parameter values, control variables, and frame are unknown.

Provenance: `assumption`. The abstract specifies 'three nearest-neighbor-coupled superconducting transmon systems' but gives no Hamiltonian; this is a commonly used generic form proposed here as a placeholder, not paper evidence.

Equation assumptions:

- Transmons modelled as Duffing oscillators truncated to a few levels.
- Nearest-neighbor coupling only (1-2 and 2-3), per the abstract wording.
- Drive enters as a local charge drive on each transmon; which transmons are driven, and whether couplings/frequencies are tunable, is unknown.

## eq_5: Gate fidelity functional and target

$$
F(\vec{\theta}) = \frac{1}{d^2}\left|\mathrm{Tr}\!\left(U_{\mathrm{Toff}}^{\dagger}\, P\,U(T;\vec{\theta})\,P\right)\right|^2,\quad d=8,\qquad \text{target: } F(\vec{\theta}^{*}) \ge 0.999
$$

Projected gate (process-type) fidelity between the realized evolution restricted to the computational subspace and the ideal Toffoli; the numerical target 99.9% is taken from the abstract, the specific functional is standard background. Leakage out of the computational subspace reduces F through the projection.

Provenance: `abstract_inferred`. The abstract states the fidelity value but not the fidelity definition; the trace-overlap gate fidelity is a standard choice and may differ from the paper's (e.g. average fidelity F_{avg} = (d F + 1)/(d+1), or a different global-phase convention).

Abstract evidence: such that the fidelity is 99.9%

Equation assumptions:

- Fidelity definition and treatment of global phase/leakage are assumed, not sourced.

## eq_6: Control optimization problem

$$
\vec{\theta}^{*} = \arg\max_{\vec{\theta}} F(\vec{\theta}) \quad \text{subject to}\quad T \approx T_{2q},\ \ \vec{\theta}\in\Theta
$$

The quantum-control task: maximize gate fidelity over admissible controls at a fixed gate time comparable to an entangling two-qubit gate time T_{2q} under the same conditions. The abstract states the optimizer is an enhanced Differential Evolution used in a 'non-greedy' manner; its update rules, enhancements, constraint set \Theta, and T_{2q} are not given.

Provenance: `abstract_inferred`. Fixed-duration fidelity maximization is the inferred optimization structure consistent with a fast single-shot gate of stated fidelity; the approximation sign reflects that only comparability of gate times is claimed.

Abstract evidence: is as fast as an entangling two-qubit gate under the same realistic conditions

Equation assumptions:

- Optimization is of fidelity at fixed T rather than, e.g., time minimization at fixed fidelity; the abstract does not specify which.

## Initial state

For the tiny verification instance: each computational basis state |c_1 c_2 t\rangle, x = 4c_1+2c_2+t \in \{0,\dots,7\}; in particular |110\rangle (x=6), whose ideal output is |111\rangle (x=7). For the control problem, the physical transmons start in their ground states with any computational-basis (or superposition) logical input.

## Measurement

For the verification instance, measure all three qubits in the computational basis after applying the gate to each basis input; the ideal outcome is deterministic: output x' = x for x \in \{0,\dots,5\}, x'=7 for x=6, x'=6 for x=7. (A full fidelity estimate would require process tomography or randomized benchmarking, not specified by the abstract.)

## Classical postprocessing

Compare measured output bit strings to the Toffoli truth table (eq_1); for simulated unitaries, compute F from eq_5. Truth-table agreement alone does not certify phases and is weaker than the 99.9% process-level fidelity claimed in the abstract.

## Algorithm steps

1. Fix the target: the ideal Toffoli unitary on registers (c_1, c_2, t) with the stated ordering and integer encoding. (eq_1, eq_2)
2. Specify the physical model: an assumed three-transmon nearest-neighbor-coupled Hamiltonian with time-dependent controls parametrized by \vec{\theta} (paper's actual model unknown). (eq_4)
3. For a candidate \vec{\theta}, propagate the system over the fixed gate time T to obtain U(T;\vec{\theta}). (eq_3)
4. Evaluate the gate fidelity of the projected propagator against the ideal Toffoli. (eq_5)
5. Maximize F over \vec{\theta} with a (non-greedy, enhanced) Differential Evolution search until F \ge 0.999 at T \approx T_{2q}; the optimizer's details are not reconstructible from the abstract. (eq_6)
6. Tiny verification instance: apply the (ideal, or optimized) gate to each of the 8 computational basis states and check the truth table of eq_1 (only x=6 and x=7 are exchanged). (eq_1)
