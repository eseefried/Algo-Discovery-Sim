# Abstract to equations

Status: **partial**

Model: `claude-fable-5-1`

Mathematics has not been independently verified.

Decompose the three-qubit Toffoli gate (equivalently the control-control-phase gate CCZ, related to Toffoli by single-qubit Hadamards on the target) into a short sequence of global (all-to-all) two-qubit entangling gates interleaved with single-qubit rotations, using no ancilla qubits or ancilla internal states; the abstract claims three global two-qubit gates suffice for Toffoli/CCZ, four for Fredkin, and seven for the conditional four-qubit phase gate.

Scope: Scoped to the smallest useful construction: the three-qubit CCZ/Toffoli gate on qubits q1,q2 (controls) and q3 (target), expressed as a sandwich of three global two-qubit entangling pulses (assumed to be Mølmer–Sørensen-type collective XX interactions, as suited to trapped ions) with unspecified local unitaries. The explicit pulse angles and local rotations of the paper are NOT available from the abstract and are left as missing information. Outside scope: the four-global-gate Fredkin construction (only its standard relation to Toffoli is recalled), the seven-gate conditional four-qubit phase gate, the five-nearest-neighbour-interaction constructions, and circuit-QED-specific implementations.

## Symbols

- $q_1, q_2, q_3$: Three qubits; q_1 and q_2 are controls, q_3 is the target. Register ordering |q_1 q_2 q_3\rangle with q_1 most significant, basis index 4q_1+2q_2+q_3.; domain: qubits, basis values in \{0,1\}
- $X_j, Y_j, Z_j$: Pauli operators acting on qubit j (identity elsewhere).; domain: 2^3\times 2^3 Hermitian unitary matrices
- $H$: Single-qubit Hadamard gate.; domain: 2\times 2 unitary
- $\mathrm{CCZ}$: Control-control-phase gate: applies a sign -1 to |111\rangle only.; domain: 8\times 8 diagonal unitary
- $\mathrm{TOF}$: Toffoli gate: flips q_3 iff q_1=q_2=1.; domain: 8\times 8 permutation unitary
- $\mathrm{FRED}$: Fredkin gate: swaps q_2 and q_3 iff q_1=1.; domain: 8\times 8 permutation unitary
- $G(\theta)$: Global two-qubit entangling gate acting on all qubit pairs simultaneously with common strength \theta (assumed Mølmer–Sørensen-type XX interaction).; domain: \theta\in[0,2\pi), 8\times 8 unitary
- $V_k$: Products of single-qubit rotations applied between global gates (k=0,\dots,3); their explicit form is not given in the abstract.; domain: elements of SU(2)^{\otimes 3}
- $N_{\mathrm{global}}$: Number of global two-qubit entangling gates in a construction.; domain: non-negative integers
- $\phi$: Irrelevant global phase.; domain: [0,2\pi)

## eq_1: Definition of CCZ

$$
\mathrm{CCZ} = \mathrm{diag}(1,1,1,1,1,1,1,-1) = I - 2\,|111\rangle\langle 111|
$$

The control-control-phase gate in the computational basis with ordering |q_1 q_2 q_3\rangle.

Provenance: `standard_background`. Standard definition of the three-qubit controlled-phase (CCZ) gate, which the abstract calls the control-control-phase (Toffoli) gate.

Equation assumptions:

- Basis ordering index 4q_1+2q_2+q_3.

## eq_2: Toffoli from CCZ via target Hadamards

$$
\mathrm{TOF} = (I\otimes I\otimes H)\,\mathrm{CCZ}\,(I\otimes I\otimes H)
$$

Exact equality relating the Toffoli gate to CCZ by conjugating the target qubit with Hadamard gates.

Provenance: `standard_background`. Standard identity H Z H = X applied to the controlled operation on the target; local gates do not change the entangling-gate count.

## eq_3: Global two-qubit entangling gate (assumed Mølmer–Sørensen form)

$$
G(\theta) = \exp\!\Big(-i\,\frac{\theta}{2}\sum_{1\le j<k\le 3} X_j X_k\Big) = \exp\!\Big(-i\,\frac{\theta}{4}\Big[\Big(\sum_{j=1}^{3} X_j\Big)^2 - 3 I\Big]\Big)
$$

A single globally addressed pulse that applies the same XX interaction to every pair of qubits simultaneously; the second form shows it is generated by the square of the collective spin operator.

Provenance: `assumption`. The abstract says only 'global two-qubit gates' implementable 'by global addressing' in ion qubits; the Mølmer–Sørensen XX form is the standard such gate, but the abstract does not specify the interaction axis, normalization, or whether all three pairs are coupled equally.

Equation assumptions:

- All three pairs are coupled with equal strength.
- Interaction axis X is a convention; any fixed axis related by local rotations is equivalent.

## eq_4: Three-global-gate structure for CCZ (claimed count, unknown parameters)

$$
\mathrm{CCZ} = e^{i\phi}\, V_3\, G(\theta_3)\, V_2\, G(\theta_2)\, V_1\, G(\theta_1)\, V_0 ,\qquad N_{\mathrm{global}}=3
$$

The claimed structure: CCZ (hence Toffoli) equals, up to global phase, a product of exactly three global entangling pulses with interleaved local unitaries V_k. The specific angles \theta_k and local rotations V_k are not given in the abstract.

Provenance: `abstract_inferred`. Inferred from the stated count of three global two-qubit gates and the statement that no ancillas are used; the sandwich form with local unitaries between global pulses is the generic shape of such ancilla-free constructions, but the parameters are the paper's contribution and are not reconstructed here.

Abstract evidence: improved circuits for the control-control-phase (Toffoli) gate and the control-swap (Fredkin) gate using three and four global two-qubit gates, respectively

Equation assumptions:

- Local single-qubit gates are not counted and may be inserted freely between global gates.
- Equality holds exactly (not approximately) up to a global phase.

## eq_5: Fredkin from Toffoli (background relation, outside the four-global-gate construction)

$$
\mathrm{FRED}_{1;2,3} = \mathrm{CNOT}_{3\to 2}\,\mathrm{TOF}_{1,2;3}\,\mathrm{CNOT}_{3\to 2}
$$

Exact standard identity expressing the Fredkin gate via one Toffoli and two CNOTs; included only to define the Fredkin target. It does NOT reproduce the paper's four-global-gate Fredkin circuit.

Provenance: `standard_background`. Standard circuit identity; the abstract's four-global-gate Fredkin construction is a separate paper contribution not recoverable from the abstract.

## eq_6: Claimed global-gate counts versus conventional counts

$$
N_{\mathrm{global}}(\mathrm{TOF})=3,\quad N_{\mathrm{global}}(\mathrm{FRED})=4,\quad N_{\mathrm{global}}(\mathrm{C^3Z})=7;\qquad N_{\mathrm{conv}}(\mathrm{TOF})=5,\quad N_{\mathrm{conv}}(\mathrm{FRED})=7
$$

Resource counts stated in the abstract: global two-qubit gates in the new constructions versus conditional two-qubit gates in conventional circuits.

Provenance: `abstract_explicit`. Directly stated counts in the abstract.

Abstract evidence: the conventional circuits, which require five (for Toffoli) and seven (for Fredkin) conditional two-qubit gates. We apply the same approach to construct the conditional four-qubit phase gate by seven global two-qubit gates

## Registers

- q_2: 1 qubits; Second control qubit.
- q_3: 1 qubits; Target qubit for CCZ/Toffoli (least significant).

## Initial state

Arbitrary three-qubit state |\psi\rangle; for truth-table verification use computational basis states |q_1 q_2 q_3\rangle, e.g. the tiny instance |110\rangle and |111\rangle, and for phase verification apply H to q_3 first so that CCZ acts as Toffoli (eq_2).

## Measurement

For verification only: prepare each computational basis state, apply the candidate circuit (with H on q_3 before and after for the Toffoli form), and measure all three qubits in the computational basis; the outcome should equal the Toffoli truth table (q_3 flipped iff q_1=q_2=1) with probability 1 for an exact decomposition.

## Classical postprocessing

Compare measured output bit strings to the expected Toffoli truth table (and, for a full check, compute the process fidelity |\mathrm{Tr}(\mathrm{CCZ}^\dagger U_{\mathrm{circuit}})|^2/64 from the reconstructed unitary) to confirm exact equality up to global phase.

## Algorithm steps

1. Define the target unitary CCZ on the three-qubit register and, if a Toffoli is desired, conjugate the target qubit q_3 by Hadamard gates. (eq_1, eq_2)
2. Choose the global entangling primitive G(\theta) available on the platform (assumed Mølmer–Sørensen-type collective XX interaction with equal pair couplings). (eq_3)
3. Apply local unitaries V_0, then alternately three global pulses G(\theta_1), G(\theta_2), G(\theta_3) interleaved with local unitaries V_1, V_2, V_3; the specific \theta_k and V_k are the paper's (unavailable) construction and must be obtained from the full paper or by numerical/analytic solution of eq_4. (eq_4)
4. (Outside scoped construction) The Fredkin gate target is defined via its standard relation to Toffoli; the paper's four-global-gate Fredkin and seven-global-gate C^3Z circuits only enter as resource counts. (eq_5, eq_6)
